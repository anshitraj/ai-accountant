Matching engine logic:

Create a service:
server/services/matchingEngine.ts

Implement:
1. Normalize names:
- lowercase
- remove punctuation
- remove Pvt Ltd, Private Limited, Ltd, LLP, Inc, Technologies, Solutions, Enterprises etc.
- trim spaces

2. Extract references from bank narration:
- UTR
- RRN
- invoice number patterns
- NEFT/RTGS/IMPS/UPI refs
- gateway settlement IDs

3. Matching rules:
Exact match:
- same amount
- same invoice number/ref/UTR/RRN OR high vendor similarity
- date within 0-3 days

Potential match:
- same amount
- vendor similarity > 0.65
- date within 7 days

Partial payment:
- bank amount less than invoice amount
- same vendor
- date within 15 days

Split payment:
- multiple bank entries sum to one invoice amount
- same vendor
- date within 15 days

Gateway settlement:
- settlement net amount equals bank credit
- allow fee/GST deductions
- date within 3 days

Payroll match:
- bank narration includes salary/payroll/employee name
- amount equals payroll net salary
- date within payroll window

Duplicate:
- same invoice number + same vendor + same amount
- same bank amount/date/narration repeated

Risk:
- no invoice for debit payment
- invoice GSTIN missing
- GST amount mismatch
- high value transaction missing document
- possible TDS category with no TDS note
- ledger marked suspense/unmapped
- low confidence match

Confidence score:
0-100
Calculate based on:
- amount match: 35 points
- date closeness: 15 points
- vendor/name similarity: 20 points
- reference/invoice/UTR match: 20 points
- source consistency: 10 points

Status thresholds:
- 85+ = Verified
- 60-84 = Potential Match / Needs Review
- below 60 = Unverified
- missing required document = Missing Invoice
- tax/compliance heuristic = Risk
